A Companion is a [[Rules/Characters/index|Character]] that operates under the control of it's leader. The companion grants its leader an [[Extra]] or [[Free]] action it can take each turn. The companion does not get its own [[Rules/Actions/index|Actions]], turns, or volition.
The Companion can take a [[Normal Move]] on each of its leader's normal turns.

A Companion starts with 
A Companion Gains +1 [[Health Points|HP]] for each its leader [[TL]].

# Companion Base

## Scout
- [[Size]] 1/2
- \[[[Health Points|4]], [[Defence|8]], [[Speed|4]], [[Seek|3]]] and \[[[Strength|-4]], [[Dexterity|2]], [[Fortitude|-1]], [[Awareness|3]]]
- This [[Companion]] can take the [[Extra]]: [[Mark]] action.

## Ravager
- [[Size]] 1
- \[[[Health Points|8]], [[Defence|11]], [[Deflection|3]], [[Sturdy|1]], [[Speed|4]], [[Seek|1]]] and \[[[Strength|2]], [[Dexterity|1]], [[Fortitude|3]], [[Awareness|1]]] or 
- \[[[Health Points|7]], [[Defence|13]], [[Speed|5]], [[Seek|1]]] and \[[[Strength|1]], [[Dexterity|3]], [[Fortitude|2]], [[Awareness|1]]]
- The Companion has two claws which are [[Natural]] [[Weapons#Dagger|Daggers]].
- The [[Companion]] can take an [[Extra]]: [[Attack]] action.

## Steed
- [[Size]] 2
- \[[[Health Points|9]], [[Defence|6]], [[Speed|5]], [[Seek|1]]] and \[[[Strength|2]], [[Dexterity|0]], [[Fortitude|4]], [[Awareness|1]]]
- The [[Companion]] can take the [[Free]]: [[Move]] action.
