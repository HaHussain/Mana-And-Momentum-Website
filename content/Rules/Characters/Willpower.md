Willpower represents your capacity to break through mental [[Strike|Strikes]].
A [[Hit]] roll must equal to your Willpower or exceed it in order to be successful. 
If the [[Hit]] roll exceeds your Willpower by 10, it is a [[Critical Strike|Crit]].

Willpower = 8 + [[Level Bonus|LB]] + [[Intelligence|INT]]
