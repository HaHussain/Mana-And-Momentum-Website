The Total Level (TL) of a [[Rules/Characters/index|Character]] is the sum of all of their [[Class Level|Class Levels]].

A Player [[Rules/Characters/index|Character]] in CREST starts at TL which is made up of being [[Class Level]] 1 in two [[Classes/index|Classes]]. As play goes on and player characters [[Levelling Up|Level Up]], they may add a level to any of their classes, eventually ending at the maximum level of TL 30.
