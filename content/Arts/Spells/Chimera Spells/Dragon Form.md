## Draconic Element

Dragon Form spells refer to a Draconic Element. A [[Rules/Characters/index|Character]] can 
- Choose from an element from a form spell list they know 
- Roll 1d6 for a random element from {[[Elemental Type|Fire]], [[Elemental Type|Water]], [[Elemental Type|Air]], [[Elemental Type|Earth]], [[Elemental Type|Light]], [[Elemental Type|Dark]]}
